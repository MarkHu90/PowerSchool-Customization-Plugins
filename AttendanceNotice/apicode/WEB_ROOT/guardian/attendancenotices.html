Please take note of the following guidelines when submitting leave requests for your child:

1. Please select accurate times for the leave. Check that the time you select from the system is the actual departure and arrival time.
If the time in the select box does not match your child's absence, please explain it in the blank space where you fill out the purpose of the leave.
2. When you see the date on your page turning from red to green, this indicates that your leave request has been processed.
3. Sick leave: Kindly provide us with the doctor's notes or medical certificate once the medical appointment is completed.
4. Personal leave: All such absences must be pre-approved by the Principal beforehand. Please submit the leave with enough advance notice (one week) to complete the process. Any leave taken without approval will be considered “unexcused leave”.
5. Please inform your child that they must check out at the receptionist in the lobby. They will give the student a “early leave pass” for them to show at the school gate.
6. The Leave request system operates from 8 am to 4:30 pm. If your leave request is submitted after 4:30 p.m., it will not be processed until the following morning.
7. Please contact us via email at hq-slo@ykpaoschool.cn or dial 61671999（ext. 9217） if you have any questions.
8. If you forget your account password on the portal, please email parentportal@ykpaoschool.cn
9. If SLO has processed your leave request and you want to submit supplementary documents, you can send an email with attachment to hq-slo@ykpaoschool.cn.

在为您的孩子提交请假申请时，请注意以下事项:

1、请选择正确的离校时间、返校时间，并检查您从系统中选择的时间是否为实际出发和到达时间。如选项里的时间与孩子实际缺勤的时间不匹配，请在请假事由中作具体说明。
2、当您看到页面上的日期由红色变为绿色时，表示您的请假申请已被处理。
3、病假：就诊完成后，请您向我们提供医生的诊断书或就诊证明。
4、事假：所有此类请假必须事先获得校长批准。请提前足够的时间（一周）提交请假申请，以便完成审批程序。任何未经批准的请假都将被视为“无故请假”。
5、请通知您的孩子必须到大厅接待员处办理出门手续。 他们会给学生一张“提前离校单”，让学生在校门口出示。
